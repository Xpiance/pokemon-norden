# Vision

## Working Title
Project Norden

## Premise
A Pokémon fan project inspired by Nordic landscapes, folklore, and atmosphere.

## Tone
Adventure, mystery, exploration.

## First Playable Goal
- Intro sequence
- Starter selection
- First town
- First route
- One trainer or rival battle
- One early milestone area

## Out of Scope For Now
- Full region
- Full dex
- Postgame
- Major custom systems
